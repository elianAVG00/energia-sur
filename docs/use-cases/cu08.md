# CU08 - View consumption trends and estimates

- **Source / status:** P and [U05 approval evidence](decisions.md#u05---user-approval-evidence);
  latest-valid-pair base and interval grouping approved; projection formula and
  residual TBDs retained. No accepted OpenSpec implementation contract or implemented
  CU is claimed. The documentary baseline is accepted under U06.
  Shared [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information) applies.
- **Actor:** account holder.
- **Preconditions:** authorized meter history; readings classified as valid under
  Q10. At least two valid readings of the same meter are needed as an estimate base.

## Main flow

1. Actor selects the personal meter/history.
2. System shows evolution by intervals between readings, with consumption in kWh
   and daily average in kWh/day for each interval.
3. Actor chooses an estimation period (allowed period limits remain TBD).
4. System uses the two latest valid readings of that meter as its estimation base.
   Numerical projection cannot be specified or implemented until its formula is approved.

## Alternatives

- Fewer than two valid readings: estimate-base prerequisite unmet; explanatory UX TBD.
- Reading validity, gaps/replacement and precision remain Q10/Q15/Q16.
- No monetary forecast, tariff model or projection formula is approved.

## Local rules and dependencies

- **CU08-R1 (P + U05, Q16):** show consumption evolution per interval between readings,
  including consumption in kWh and daily average in kWh/day, not a merged-only total.
- **CU08-R2 (U05, Q15):** use the two latest valid readings of the same meter for the
  estimate base. The projection formula remains TBD; do not infer linear extrapolation.
- Dependencies: CU04/CU05 history, auth/persistence, backend calculations;
  [Q03](decisions.md#q03), [Q10](decisions.md#q10), [Q15](decisions.md#q15),
  [Q16](decisions.md#q16); Q00. CU06 bills are not a stated estimate prerequisite.

## Verifiable scenario candidates

Fixtures stipulate valid readings and exact elapsed days; they do not decide
reading validation, global timezone, sub-day precision or rounding policy.

### CU08-S01 - Evolution grouped by reading interval [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU08-R1; [Q16](decisions.md#q16), [Q10](decisions.md#q10).
**Given** valid meter M readings of 1000, 1020 and 1080 kWh on 2026-11-01, 2026-11-03
and 2026-11-07, with elapsed intervals of exactly 2 and 4 days, **When** evolution
is viewed, **Then** it includes these separate interval results:

| Interval | Consumption | Daily average |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-03 | 20 kWh | 10 kWh/day |
| 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-07 | 60 kWh | 15 kWh/day |

This daily-average fixture does not select a formula for projecting future consumption.

### CU08-S02 - Latest valid same-meter base [APPROVED base; projection BLOCKED/TBD]
**Trace:** CU08-R2; [Q15](decisions.md#q15), [Q16](decisions.md#q16), [Q10](decisions.md#q10).
**Given** M's three valid readings from CU08-S01 and another meter's later reading
dated 2026-11-08, **When** an estimate base is selected for M, **Then** it is M's
1020 kWh reading on 2026-11-03 and 1080 kWh reading on 2026-11-07, not M's older
reading or the other meter's reading. No projected kWh result is asserted: the
formula remains Q15 and chosen-period limits remain Q16, both TBD.

### CU08-S03 - Insufficient valid readings [PLANNED; UX REVIEW]
