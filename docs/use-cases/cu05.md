# CU05 - Record a meter reading with a photo

- **Source / status:** P, CU05 in the [source key](README.md); PLANNED implementation,
  accepted U06 documentary baseline, not implemented. Shared [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information),
  [SR04](shared-rules.md#sr04---user-supplied-personal-evidence) apply.
- **Actor:** account holder.
- **Preconditions:** personal address/meter association and authorized access;
  approved reading validation and synthetic photo handling for tests.

## Main flow

1. Actor selects the personal meter and supplies reading date and accumulated kWh.
2. Actor attaches a meter photo.
3. Actor reviews and explicitly confirms the value.
4. System incorporates the confirmed reading in that meter's history.

## Alternatives

- Before confirmation, no incorporation into history (P); draft retention is TBD.
- Invalid photo, duplicate/decreasing reading or replaced meter: Q10/Q11, not an
  implicit OCR or rollover policy.

## Local rules and dependencies

- **CU05-R1 (P):** record date, accumulated kWh and photo; confirm the value before
  incorporating it into history. Manual confirmation is not approval for OCR.
- Dependencies: CU04 associations, auth/persistence, approved file handling;
  [Q03](decisions.md#q03), [Q09](decisions.md#q09), [Q10](decisions.md#q10),
  [Q11](decisions.md#q11); Q00.

## Verifiable scenario candidates

### CU05-S01 - Confirm before incorporation [PLANNED]
**Trace:** CU05-R1; [SR04](shared-rules.md#sr04---user-supplied-personal-evidence).
**Given** a valid synthetic meter/date/value/photo entry awaiting review, **When**
the actor has not confirmed it, **Then** it is not yet part of reading history;
**When** the actor confirms it, **Then** that confirmed value is incorporated for
the selected meter. Input-validity details await Q10/Q11.

### CU05-S02 - Unsupported photo [BLOCKED/TBD]
**Trace:** CU05-R1; [Q11](decisions.md#q11).
**Given** a synthetic attachment outside the approved photo policy, **When** the
actor attempts confirmation, **Then** validation follows that policy. Formats,
limits, storage and exact rejection behavior have not been approved.

### CU05-S03 - Reading below an earlier value [BLOCKED/TBD]
**Trace:** CU05-R1; [Q10](decisions.md#q10).
**Given** an earlier accumulated reading and a later lower one, **When** the actor
submits it, **Then** the approved validation/replacement/rollover rule determines
the result. Do not assume automatic rejection or correction.
