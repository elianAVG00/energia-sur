# CU09 - Record and query an interruption diary

- **Source / status:** P, [U05](decisions.md#u05---user-approval-evidence) and
  [U06](decisions.md#u06---baseline-acceptance-and-cu09-proposal-authorization).
  Accepted documentary baseline; **SELECTED first development for stage 06**.
  [Proposal](../../openspec/changes/record-cu09-interruption-diary/proposal.md) and
  [design](../../openspec/changes/record-cu09-interruption-diary/design.md) await review;
  no implementation or Apply authorization is claimed. Shared
  [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information) and
  [SR04](shared-rules.md#sr04---user-supplied-personal-evidence) apply.
- **Actor:** account holder with a registered domicile.
- **Preconditions:** Q00 satisfied on user confirmation; authenticated account and
  own registered domicile, with implementation design decisions still pending.

## Main flow

1. Actor selects a domicile registered in their own account and supplies start,
   optional end, optional observations and optional complaint number.
2. System validates Buenos Aires minute-resolution dates: no future cut date;
   any supplied end must be strictly after start. Omitted end means ongoing.
3. The same account may consult, correct open/closed cuts under the same validation,
   close an ongoing cut and export its own domicile's records.
4. For a queried period, count only positive-duration intersections. Open cuts use
   min(summary generation, period end) as effective end, without stored closure.
5. Keep overlapping entries but count shared time once per domicile; display total
   hours and minutes. Exclude entries with no duration inside the period.
6. Export a PDF with required fields, a table ordered by start descending, and
   “En curso” in the end column when closure is absent. No complaint is submitted.

## Alternatives

- No domicile/another account's domicile: cannot save; other-account consult,
  correction, closure and export are forbidden as well.
- Future start/end, equal end/start or earlier end: reject creation/correction/closure.
- Report effective end does not close stored records. Reopening by clearing a closed
  end, reassignment/deletion and concurrency mechanics are not decided by this fiche.
- Auth mechanism, persistence and PDF technology require explicit design approval.

## Local rules and dependencies

- **CU09-R1 (P):** diary supports start, optional end, observations, optional complaint
  number and ongoing interruptions.
- **CU09-R2 (P):** period query and PDF records/hours support a complaint, not its submission.
- **CU09-R3 (U05 + U06, Q18):** own registered domicile required; only that account
  may save/view/correct/close/export its cuts. No household sharing is authorized.
- **CU09-R4 (U05, Q19):** keep both overlapping records and count their union per domicile.
- **CU09-R5 (U05 + U06, Q20):** clip to queried period; use min(generation, period end)
  for open cuts, exclude nonpositive intersections, display total hours and minutes.
- **CU09-R6 (U05 + U06, Q23):** PDF domicile/period, each cut's dates/times/status,
  total hours/minutes and supplied observations/complaint number; table start descending,
  missing end exactly “En curso”. Remaining presentation/generation details need design review.
- **CU09-R7 (U06, Q17):** Buenos Aires local time, recorded to the minute, no future
  cut dates, and supplied end strictly after start; same validation for every mutation.
- **CU09-R8 (U06, Q17/Q18):** owner can correct both open/closed cuts and close ongoing
  cuts. Summary-generation time is a calculation boundary, never an implicit closure.
- **Minimum CU04 dependency:** register, list and select an account-owned domicile
  with stable identity and display address. No meter, GeoRef, domicile editing/deletion,
  sharing or full CU04 implementation is required by the diary. Proposed delivery
  scope and unresolved identity validation are explicit in design D0/D2 and Q08/Q09.
- Dependencies: [Q03](decisions.md#q03) auth, Q08 identity, Q09 lifecycle,
  [Q17](decisions.md#q17), [Q18](decisions.md#q18), [Q19](decisions.md#q19),
  [Q20](decisions.md#q20), [Q23](decisions.md#q23); Q00 approved, not an Apply gate.

## Verifiable scenario candidates

Synthetic fixtures use Buenos Aires local time at minute resolution. A stated
generation/validation clock is fixed, not the test runner's live clock. All operations
are by the domicile's account unless the scenario explicitly tests another account.
These are approved documentary outcomes, not executed tests.

### CU09-S01 - Save a closed interruption for own domicile [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R1, CU09-R3, CU09-R7; [Q17](decisions.md#q17), [Q18](decisions.md#q18).
**Given** account A's registered domicile D, validation now 2026-11-07 12:00, start
2026-11-01 10:00 and end 12:00, **When** A saves with D and no complaint number,
**Then** the closed cut is associated with D without requiring that optional number.

### CU09-S02 - Record an ongoing interruption [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R1, CU09-R3, CU09-R5, CU09-R7; [Q17](decisions.md#q17), [Q20](decisions.md#q20).
**Given** own domicile D and a valid past start, **When** a cut is saved without an
end, **Then** it is ongoing; generating a summary leaves its stored end absent.

### CU09-S03 - Own-account domicile is mandatory [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R3; [Q18](decisions.md#q18),
[SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information).
**Given** account A with D and account B with E, **When** A saves an otherwise valid
cut, **Then** selecting D saves against D, no selection does not save, and selecting
E registered only in B does not save. Auth/error-contract technology awaits design review.

### CU09-S04 - Keep overlapping records and union their time [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R4, CU09-R5; [Q19](decisions.md#q19), [Q20](decisions.md#q20).
**Given** D's 2026-11-01 cuts 10:00-12:00 and 11:00-13:00, **When** that day's
summary is generated after 13:00, **Then** both records remain and total is
**3 h 0 min**, counting 11:00-12:00 once; records are not merged or rejected.

### CU09-S05 - Clip a closed cut to November [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R5; [Q20](decisions.md#q20).
**Given** D's cut 2026-10-31 23:00 to 2026-11-01 02:00 and period 2026-11-01 00:00
to 2026-12-01 00:00, **When** summarized after the cut, **Then** it is included with
**2 h 0 min**, not its full three hours.

### CU09-S06 - PDF fields and union total [APPROVED content; technology pending]
**Trace:** CU09-R2, CU09-R4, CU09-R5, CU09-R6; [Q23](decisions.md#q23), [Q18](decisions.md#q18).
**Given** D's two closed cuts from CU09-S04, period 2026-11-01 00:00 to 2026-11-02
00:00, first cut observation “Synthetic note” and complaint “TEST-001”, neither on
the second, **When** the owner exports, **Then** PDF includes D, period, both cuts'
dates/times/closed status, supplied note/number and **3 h 0 min** total. Both rows
remain; the 11:00 start precedes 10:00. Missing optional fields do not remove a row.

### CU09-S07 - Ongoing cut capped at fixed generation time [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R5, CU09-R6; [Q20](decisions.md#q20), [Q23](decisions.md#q23).
**Given** ongoing start 2026-11-01 10:00, period that day 00:00 to next day 00:00,
and fixed generation time 2026-11-01 15:00, **When** summary/PDF is generated,
**Then** effective end is 15:00, total is **5 h 0 min**, state stays ongoing and
the PDF end column is exactly “En curso”; no end is stored by reporting.

### CU09-S08 - Ongoing cut capped at earlier period end [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R5; [Q20](decisions.md#q20).
**Given** ongoing start 2026-11-01 22:00, period 2026-11-01 00:00 to 2026-11-02
00:00 and fixed generation 2026-11-02 03:00, **When** summarized, **Then** effective end is 00:00,
contribution is **2 h 0 min**, not 5 hours, and stored state remains ongoing.

### CU09-S09 - Exclusive owner account for all five operations [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R3, CU09-R8; [Q18](decisions.md#q18), [Q03](decisions.md#q03).
**Given** D belongs only to A and B is another account, **When** each account tries
save, consult, correct, close and export on D, **Then** A is authorized subject to
the same date rules, while B is forbidden for every action and receives no diary
records or PDF. Exact auth mechanism/status codes await design approval.

### CU09-S10 - Reject future or nonpositive cut dates [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R7; [Q17](decisions.md#q17).
**Given** fixed validation now 2026-11-07 12:00 in Buenos Aires and own D,
**When** creating/correcting/closing with these dates, **Then** reject each invalid case:

| Start on 2026-11-07 | Supplied end on that day | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12:01 | Absent | Future start |
| 10:00 | 12:01 | Future end |
| 10:00 | 10:00 | End not after start |
| 10:00 | 09:59 | End before start |

Invalid correction/closure does not replace the valid stored record.

### CU09-S11 - Correct both open and closed cuts [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R7, CU09-R8; [Q17](decisions.md#q17), [Q18](decisions.md#q18).
**Given** owner A, now 2026-11-07 12:00, an open cut and a separate closed cut,
**When** A corrects the open start to 2026-11-01 09:45 (end still absent) and the
closed interval to 2026-11-01 10:15-11:45, **Then** both valid corrections persist.
Both are rejected instead if they use any invalid CU09-S10 dates; owner rules apply
to both states. This does not decide reopening a closed cut by clearing its end.

### CU09-S12 - Owner closes an ongoing cut [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R7, CU09-R8; [Q17](decisions.md#q17), [Q18](decisions.md#q18).
**Given** A's ongoing cut from 2026-11-07 10:00 and fixed now 12:00 that day,
**When** A supplies end 10:30, **Then** the cut is closed with that stored end and
its contribution to that day is **0 h 30 min**; future/nonpositive ends are rejected.

### CU09-S13 - Exclude zero-intersection records [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R5; [Q20](decisions.md#q20).
**Given** queried 2026-11-01 00:00 to 2026-11-02 00:00 and valid cuts ending exactly
at its start, starting exactly at its end, or wholly outside it, **When** summarized
after those cuts, **Then** none appears in the summary/PDF and their contribution
is **0 h 0 min**. Their positive-duration stored intervals are not deleted.

### CU09-S14 - Minute precision and hours/minutes total [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R4, CU09-R5, CU09-R7; [Q17](decisions.md#q17), [Q20](decisions.md#q20).
**Given** D's valid 2026-11-01 cuts 10:05-10:50 and 10:35-11:10 in Buenos Aires,
**When** that day's summary is generated after 11:10, **Then** recorded times retain
those minutes and the union total is **1 h 5 min**, not the sum of both durations.

### CU09-S15 - PDF table order and ongoing end text [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU09-R5, CU09-R6; [Q20](decisions.md#q20), [Q23](decisions.md#q23).
**Given** D's cut 2026-11-01 10:00-11:00 and ongoing cut starting that day 12:00,
period that whole day and fixed generation 13:30, **When** A exports, **Then** the
table shows the 12:00 cut before 10:00, the ongoing row's end is “En curso”, the
closed row's end is 11:00, both statuses are shown, and total is **2 h 30 min**.
