# CU02 - Consultar trabajos de mantenimiento eléctrico informados por ENRE

- **Source / status:** P, CU02 in the [source key](README.md); **COMPLETED / ARCHIVED**
  (U22, 2026-11-10), archived change
  `2026-11-10-implement-scheduled-works-cu02`, main spec
  [scheduled-works](../../openspec/specs/scheduled-works/spec.md). Shared
  [SR01](shared-rules.md#sr01---public-access),
  [SR03](shared-rules.md#sr03---provenance-and-uncertainty) apply.
- **Actor:** visitor or account holder.
- **Preconditions:** a supported partido/localidad or an address/position resolvable
  through GeoRef, and a valid ENRE snapshot.

## Definitive scope

Public consultation of the maintenance works ENRE reports for Edesur: partido/localidad,
feeder (`subestacion_alimentador`), affected users, the published restoration estimate
when present, provenance, three-valued coverage and availability states. Address and
GPS assistance reuse the CU01 GeoRef resolution. No date, reason or distance is invented.

**Out of scope by design:** future agenda, temporal filters, planned start/end,
unpublished reasons and distances. They are not pending tasks.

## Main flow

1. Actor chooses a partido (with an optional localidad), or resolves an address/position.
2. System serves the latest valid ENRE snapshot; it never queries ENRE at request time.
3. Actor consults the works with feeder, affected users, the published restoration
   estimate, source/update context, coverage and availability.

## Alternatives

- No works, unresolved zone, unavailable or stale source, and unverified coverage are
  distinct states; no negative result or future work is invented.

## Local rules and dependencies

- **CU02-R1 (definitive, U22):** query by partido with an optional localidad; show the
  published maintenance works, feeder, affected users, restoration estimate, provenance,
  coverage and availability; assist with address/GPS. The out-of-scope items above are
  not required by this rule.
- Dependencies: CU01 ENRE transport, GeoRef and coverage catalog reused read-only; CU10
  remains separate.

## Verifiable scenarios (implemented)

### CU02-S01 - Query reported maintenance works [IMPLEMENTED]
**Trace:** CU02-R1; [SR01](shared-rules.md#sr01---public-access),
[SR03](shared-rules.md#sr03---provenance-and-uncertainty).
**Given** a fresh snapshot with a maintenance work for ALMIRANTE BROWN/GLEW,
**When** a visitor queries that partido and localidad without an account,
**Then** the work, feeder, affected users, published restoration text, source and
update date are visible.

### CU02-S02 - No works vs unverified coverage [IMPLEMENTED]
**Trace:** CU02-R1; [SR03](shared-rules.md#sr03---provenance-and-uncertainty).
**Given** a fresh snapshot with no rows for covered BERAZATEGUI/BERAZATEGUI and,
separately, an area absent from the catalog,
**When** each area is queried,
**Then** the first returns "no works" and the second returns "coverage unverified"
without claiming that no works exist.

### CU02-S03 - Address and GPS assistance [IMPLEMENTED]
**Trace:** CU02-R1, [Q02](decisions.md#q02).
**Given** an ambiguous address or a consented device position,
**When** the actor searches,
**Then** the system presents candidates or degrades to the resolved partido before
querying, and the browser never calls GeoRef or ENRE directly.

### CU02-S04 - Unavailable source and freshness [IMPLEMENTED]
**Trace:** CU02-R1; [SR03](shared-rules.md#sr03---provenance-and-uncertainty),
[Q07](decisions.md#q07).
**Given** a stale or failed refresh, or no valid snapshot,
**When** an area is queried,
**Then** the last valid snapshot is served with its freshness or an explicit
unavailable state; no future work and no "no works" result is fabricated.

## Historical original proposal (preserved, not the delivered scope)

The original academic proposal described "Query upcoming scheduled works" filtered by
area and dates, showing planned start/end, reason and proximity. It is preserved below
for traceability and is **not** a pending requirement of the delivered CU02.

### Original main flow
1. Actor chooses an area and date range.
2. System retrieves matching upcoming scheduled works from acquired public data.
3. Actor consults start, end, reason and proximity, with source/update context.

### Original local rule
- **CU02-R1 (original):** filter by area and dates; show planned start/end, reason and
  proximity.

### Original scenario candidates
- **CU02-S01 (historical):** with in-range and out-of-range official works, a visitor
  selects area and dates and sees start/end, reason and proximity. Not delivered.
- **CU02-S02 (historical):** an unresolved location or failed refresh must not fabricate
  a fresh result. This concern is delivered by the fail-closed states above.
- **CU02-S03 (historical):** a date/proximity boundary rule. Not delivered; no boundary
  or distance threshold is defined.

**Traceability:** [U22](decisions.md#u22---cu02-definitive-scope-redefinition) records the
definitive scope; [U21](decisions.md#u21---cu02-reduced-scope-and-propose-authorization)
is preserved as the historical reduction. The redefinition is a user decision; no
teacher approval of it is claimed.
