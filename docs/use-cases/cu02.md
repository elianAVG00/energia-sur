# CU02 - Query upcoming scheduled works

**Implemented demo (U21):** *CU02 — Consultar trabajos programados informados por ENRE.*
The original academic use case (upcoming works filtered by dates, with start/end,
reason and proximity) is preserved below and is **not** claimed as fully implemented.

- **Source / status:** P, CU02 in the [source key](README.md); **IMPLEMENTED / VERIFIED**
  for the **U21 reduced scope** (change `implement-scheduled-works-cu02`), not synced or
  archived. ENRE publishes reported maintenance works without dates, reason or
  coordinates, so date filtering, proximity and the upcoming/in-progress split are
  explicitly not implemented; see
  [U21](decisions.md#u21---cu02-reduced-scope-and-propose-authorization).
  Shared [SR01](shared-rules.md#sr01---public-access),
  [SR03](shared-rules.md#sr03---provenance-and-uncertainty) apply.
- **Implemented scope:** public query by partido with optional localidad, address/GPS
  assistance, published partido/localidad/alimentador/affected users, estimated
  restoration text when present, provenance, three-valued coverage and availability
  states. No date filters, no future agenda, no invented start/end, reason or distance.
- **Actor:** visitor or account holder.
- **Preconditions:** supported area/date inputs and official scheduled-work data;
  period and proximity definitions approved before exact filtering is implemented.

## Main flow

1. Actor chooses an area and date range.
2. System retrieves matching upcoming scheduled works from acquired public data.
3. Actor consults start, end, reason and proximity, with source/update context.

**Reduced demo:** the implemented flow selects an area (no date range) and shows the
reported works with source/update context; start, end, reason and proximity are not
published and are presented as limitations.

## Alternatives

- No works, unresolved area or unavailable source: distinguish no matching records
  from missing information; the exact UX is a review candidate under Q02/Q07.
- Boundary dates and proximity cannot be determined until Q05 is answered.

## Local rules and dependencies

- **CU02-R1 (P):** filter by area and dates; show planned start/end, reason and proximity.
- **U21 limitation:** the operational ENRE feed exposes in-progress maintenance works
  (partido, localidad, alimentador, usuarios, estimated restoration) and no future
  agenda; Edesur's dated agenda is unavailable. CU02-R1's dates, start/end, reason and
  proximity remain unsatisfied and must not be presented as working behavior.
- Dependencies: CU10 acquisition, official coverage and location mapping shared with
  CU01, without requiring the actor to run CU01 first. [Q02](decisions.md#q02),
  [Q04](decisions.md#q04), [Q05](decisions.md#q05), [Q07](decisions.md#q07); Q00.

## Verifiable scenario candidates

### CU02-S01 - Filter scheduled works [PARTIAL - reduced demo]
**Trace:** CU02-R1; [SR01](shared-rules.md#sr01---public-access),
[SR03](shared-rules.md#sr03---provenance-and-uncertainty).
**Given** synthetic in-range and out-of-range official works for supported areas,
**When** a visitor selects area and dates, **Then** matching works show start/end,
reason, proximity, source and update date without login. Exact set awaits Q04/Q05.
**Implemented part:** area/source/update date without login, plus alimentador, affected
users and the published restoration text. **Excluded:** dates, start/end, reason and
proximity.

### CU02-S02 - Unavailable data or location [IMPLEMENTED - fail-closed states]
**Trace:** [SR03](shared-rules.md#sr03---provenance-and-uncertainty),
[Q02](decisions.md#q02), [Q07](decisions.md#q07).
**Given** an unresolved location or failed public-source refresh, **When** works
are queried, **Then** no fresh successful result is fabricated; exact error/empty
presentation awaits an approved policy.

### CU02-S03 - Work at a date/proximity boundary [NOT IMPLEMENTED - BLOCKED]
**Trace:** CU02-R1; [Q05](decisions.md#q05).
**Given** a work starting at a chosen date boundary or proximity limit, **When**
filters are applied, **Then** inclusion follows the approved boundary/distance rule.
There is no accepted inclusivity or distance threshold yet.
