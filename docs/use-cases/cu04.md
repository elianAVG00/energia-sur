# CU04 - Manage personal addresses and meters

- **Source / status:** P, CU04 in the [source key](README.md); implemented, verified
  and archived after U11/U12. Shared [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information),
  [SR04](shared-rules.md#sr04---user-supplied-personal-evidence) apply.
- **Actor:** account holder.
- **Preconditions:** an authenticated account and personal-record persistence.
  CU09 implements the account-owned domicile prerequisite; CU04 extends it.

## Main flow

1. Actor supplies a personal address and meter information.
2. System saves the association to that account using the approved identity model.
3. Actor consults or modifies the saved information.
4. Saved addresses/meters can be associated with later queries and personal records.

## Alternatives

- Duplicate domicile addresses remain allowed with a nonblocking UI warning. U11/U12
  decide CU04 meter identity and per-account duplicate rules; legal address validation
  is not implied. Physical replacement and reassignment are not included.
- Corrections retain IDs and CU09 cut associations. Future PDFs use the current
  address. Deletion, transfer and linked-file lifecycle remain outside this change.

## Local rules and dependencies

- **CU04-R1 (P):** save/modify personal addresses and meters for queries, readings,
  bills and interruptions. U11/U12 decide CU04 ownership, cardinality, identity,
  corrections and input rules; other CUs' lifecycle and file policies remain open.
- Dependencies: account/auth, PostgreSQL integration and identity/lifecycle decisions;
  GeoRef as an address aid, not proof of ownership. [Q03](decisions.md#q03),
  [Q08](decisions.md#q08), [Q09](decisions.md#q09); Q00.
- CU09's implemented and archived prerequisite is account-owned domicile registration,
  listing and selection with stable identity and display address, without a meter.
  The [archived CU04 change](../../openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-08-manage-cu04-domiciles-meters/) extends it after verification.

## Verifiable scenario candidates

### CU04-S01 - Save an address and meter [PLANNED; CU04 rules approved U11/U12]
**Trace:** CU04-R1; [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information).
**Given** an authenticated synthetic account and valid address/meter input,
**When** the actor saves them, **Then** the association can be consulted for that
account and selected for later personal records. CU04 identity rules are in Q03/Q08;
later personal-record behavior requires its own accepted contract.

### CU04-S02 - Modify linked information [PLANNED; CU04 rules approved U11/U12]
**Trace:** CU04-R1; [Q08](decisions.md#q08), [Q09](decisions.md#q09).
**Given** a meter/address with linked history, **When** the actor changes its details,
**Then** IDs and existing CU09 cut associations remain unchanged; future PDFs show
the current address. Other linked-history, reassignment and deletion rules remain open.

### CU04-S03 - Cross-account access [PLANNED; CU04 rules approved U11/U12]
**Trace:** [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information),
[Q03](decisions.md#q03).
**Given** two unrelated synthetic accounts, **When** one requests the other's
personal address or meter, **Then** private records are not exposed and a foreign
or missing resource returns 404. Session, Origin and CSRF checks follow U11.
