# CU07 - Compare recorded consumption with a bill

- **Source / status:** P + U05; matching revised and approved under
  [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization) on
  2026-11-09. Implemented, verified and archived as
  [2026-11-09-compare-cu07-consumption-with-bill](../../openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-09-compare-cu07-consumption-with-bill/proposal.md),
  with the [main specification](../../openspec/specs/bill-comparisons/spec.md).
  Shared [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information) applies.
- **Actor:** account holder.
- **Preconditions:** an authorized CU06 bill and CU05 readings for the same owned
  meter and account, with at least one candidate reading inside each boundary window.

## Main flow

1. Actor selects a bill for a personal meter.
2. System finds CU05 readings of the same meter whose
   `America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires` local date is within ±7 days inclusive of each
   billed-period boundary.
3. If exactly one reading lies on each exact boundary date, the system compares
   directly.
4. Otherwise, the system shows the real reading dates and requires explicit
   confirmation before comparing an approximate period.
5. If several candidate readings exist for a boundary, the actor manually selects the
   start and end readings.
6. System obtains recorded consumption as end reading minus start reading in kWh and
   shows recorded consumption, billed kWh, the exact difference and whether recorded
   or billed consumption is higher.
7. If a compatible reading is missing or the end reading is lower than the start
   reading, the system does not calculate a comparison.

## Alternatives

- No candidate inside a boundary window: no comparison; no interpolation, tolerance
  beyond ±7 days or proration.
- More than one candidate: manual selection; candidates are never averaged, merged or
  interpolated.
- A reading 8 or more days from a boundary: not a candidate.
- End reading lower than the start reading: no comparison, neutral message.
- Different meter: its readings cannot satisfy the comparison precondition.
- No percentage, warning threshold, tariff or fraud claim is added.
- CU07 is read-only: no new tables, migrations, PDF extraction or mutation of CU05/CU06
  records.

## Local rules and dependencies

- **CU07-R1 (U14, supersedes Q13):** compare only CU05 readings of the bill's meter and
  owning account. A reading is a candidate when its Buenos Aires local date is within
  ±7 days inclusive of the boundary. Exact boundary dates compare directly; other
  in-window pairs need explicit confirmation. No interpolation, proration or
  estimation. Missing candidates or a decreasing end reading yield no comparison.
- **CU07-R2 (U05 + U14, Q14):** display recorded consumption, billed kWh and the exact
  difference in kWh, identifying recorded-higher, billed-higher or no-difference.
  Percentage is explicitly excluded; exact wording/rounding remains TBD.
- **CU07-R3 (U14):** when multiple candidates exist, the actor selects the start and
  end readings. The backend validates the selected IDs, ownership, window membership
  and confirmation; it does not rely only on the confirmation modal.
- Dependencies: CU04/CU05/CU06, auth/persistence; [Q03](decisions.md#q03),
  [Q10](decisions.md#q10), [Q12](decisions.md#q12), [Q13](decisions.md#q13),
  [Q14](decisions.md#q14); academic gate Q00.

## Verifiable scenario candidates

### CU07-S01 - Exact dates and recorded consumption higher [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R2; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization),
[Q13](decisions.md#q13), [Q14](decisions.md#q14).
**Given** meter M's bill for 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-07 with 100 billed kWh and valid M
readings of 1000 and 1120 kWh on those exact local dates, **When** comparison is
requested, **Then** recorded consumption is 120 kWh, the difference is 20 kWh, and
recorded consumption is identified as higher. No percentage is shown.

### CU07-S02 - Boundary tolerance of 7 versus 8 days [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization).
**Given** meter M's bill for 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-07, **When** comparison is
requested, **Then**:

| Same-meter reading dates | Distance from boundary | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-25 (start) and 2026-11-14 (end) | 7 days each | Candidate; approximate comparison after confirmation |
| 2026-10-24 (start only) | 8 days | Not a candidate; no comparison without another compatible reading |

Exact dates 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-07 compare directly without confirmation.

### CU07-S03 - Different meters [APPROVED RULE; error UX TBD]
**Trace:** CU07-R1; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization),
[SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information).
**Given** a bill for M and only another meter's readings inside the windows,
**When** comparison is requested, **Then** no comparison is calculated from those
readings and the other meter's data is not exposed.

### CU07-S04 - Billed consumption higher [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R2; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization),
[Q14](decisions.md#q14).
**Given** valid same-meter readings of 1000 and 1080 kWh exactly on a bill's boundaries
and 100 billed kWh, **When** comparison is requested, **Then** recorded consumption is
80 kWh, the difference is 20 kWh, and billed consumption is identified as higher.

### CU07-S05 - Equal consumption [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R2; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization),
[Q14](decisions.md#q14).
**Given** valid same-meter readings of 1000 and 1100 kWh on a bill's boundary dates and
100 billed kWh, **When** comparison is requested, **Then** the difference is 0 kWh and
the result states that there is no difference.

### CU07-S06 - Multiple candidates require manual selection [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R3; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization).
**Given** a bill and two candidate readings inside one boundary window, **When**
comparison is requested, **Then** the actor must select one start and one end reading;
the system uses exactly those values and does not merge or interpolate them.

### CU07-S07 - Approximate comparison requires confirmation [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R3; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization).
**Given** an in-window pair whose local dates differ from the billed boundaries,
**When** comparison is requested without confirmation, **Then** no result is returned;
**When** the actor explicitly confirms, **Then** the result shows both real dates and a
visible warning that the periods differ.

### CU07-S08 - Zero recorded consumption [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R2; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization).
**Given** two distinct same-meter readings with the same value inside their windows
and a bill for 100 kWh, **When** comparison is requested, **Then** recorded consumption
is 0 kWh, the difference is 100 kWh and billed consumption is higher; no percentage is
computed.

### CU07-S09 - Decreasing reading prevents calculation [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1; [U14](decisions.md#u14---cu07-revised-matching-and-comparison-authorization).
**Given** a start reading of 1200 kWh and an end reading of 1100 kWh inside their
windows, **When** comparison is requested, **Then** no consumption or difference is
calculated and a neutral decreasing-reading outcome is shown.

### CU07-S10 - Cross-account isolation [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R3, [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information).
**Given** account B does not own meter M, **When** B requests candidates or a
comparison for M's bill or readings, **Then** access is denied without disclosing bill,
reading or meter data.
