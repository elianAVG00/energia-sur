# CU07 - Compare recorded consumption with a bill

- **Source / status:** P and [U05 approval evidence](decisions.md#u05---user-approval-evidence);
  matching and difference rules approved, residual TBDs retained. No accepted
  OpenSpec implementation contract or implemented CU is claimed. The documentary
  baseline is accepted under U06. Shared
  [SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information) applies.
- **Actor:** account holder.
- **Preconditions:** authorized bill and readings classified as valid under Q10,
  for the same meter, exactly on both billed-period boundary dates.

## Main flow

1. Actor selects a bill for a personal meter.
2. System checks for valid same-meter readings whose dates exactly match the billed
   start and end dates; without both matches it does not calculate a comparison.
3. System obtains recorded consumption as end reading minus start reading in kWh.
4. System shows the kWh difference and whether recorded or billed consumption is
   higher, or states that there is no difference when they coincide.

## Alternatives

- Missing either exact-date match: no comparison; no tolerance/interpolation/proration.
- Different meter: its readings cannot satisfy the comparison precondition.
- Reading validity, duplicate-boundary readings and replacement handling remain Q10;
  detailed display/rounding and error UX remain unapproved. No fraud claim is added.

## Local rules and dependencies

- **CU07-R1 (P + U05, Q13):** compare only valid readings from the bill's meter on
  exactly its start/end dates; otherwise do not calculate the comparison.
- **CU07-R2 (U05, Q14):** display difference in kWh with recorded-higher,
  billed-higher or no-difference outcome; exact wording/rounding remain TBD.
- Dependencies: CU04/CU05/CU06, auth/persistence and backend calculation;
  [Q03](decisions.md#q03), [Q10](decisions.md#q10), [Q12](decisions.md#q12),
  [Q13](decisions.md#q13), [Q14](decisions.md#q14); academic gate Q00.

## Verifiable scenario candidates

Fixtures below assume already-valid readings and well-defined bill dates; they
do not approve Q10 validation, timestamp precision or Q12's storage representation.

### CU07-S01 - Exact dates and recorded consumption higher [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R2; [Q13](decisions.md#q13), [Q14](decisions.md#q14).
**Given** meter M's bill for 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-07 with 100 billed kWh and valid
M readings of 1000 and 1120 kWh on those exact dates, **When** comparison is requested,
**Then** recorded consumption is 120 kWh, the difference is 20 kWh, and recorded
consumption is identified as higher. No percentage or warning threshold is implied.

### CU07-S02 - Missing exact boundary match [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1; [Q13](decisions.md#q13).
**Given** meter M's bill for 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-07 and the following valid readings,
**When** comparison is requested for each case, **Then** no comparison is calculated:

| Same-meter reading dates | Missing exact match | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-07 | Start | No comparison |
| 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-08 | End | No comparison |
| 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-08 | Both | No comparison |

Nearby readings do not authorize interpolation, tolerance or proration.

### CU07-S03 - Different meters [APPROVED RULE; error UX TBD]
**Trace:** CU07-R1; [Q13](decisions.md#q13),
[SR02](shared-rules.md#sr02---account-bound-personal-information).
**Given** a bill for M and only another meter's readings on its exact boundary dates,
**When** comparison is requested, **Then** no comparison is calculated from those
readings. Exact error wording/selection UX is not approved by this outcome.

### CU07-S04 - Billed consumption higher [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R2; [Q13](decisions.md#q13), [Q14](decisions.md#q14).
**Given** valid same-meter readings of 1000 and 1080 kWh exactly on a bill's start/end
dates and 100 billed kWh, **When** comparison is requested, **Then** recorded
consumption is 80 kWh, the difference is 20 kWh, and billed consumption is higher.

### CU07-S05 - Equal consumption [APPROVED RULE]
**Trace:** CU07-R1, CU07-R2; [Q13](decisions.md#q13), [Q14](decisions.md#q14).
**Given** valid same-meter readings of 1000 and 1100 kWh exactly on a bill's start/end
dates and 100 billed kWh, **When** comparison is requested, **Then** the difference
is 0 kWh and the result states that there is no difference.
